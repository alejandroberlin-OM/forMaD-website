How it works

Three steps. Seconds, not hours.

Step 01
Create your profile

Set up your profile once: name, CPSO number, hospital, contact details. Every form auto-fills the physician's Section 1 from that moment forward. Group practices share a central provider directory so every physician in the group is always up to date.

Provider directory shared across your group
Step 02
Add context. Paste your notes.

Tell forMaD the key context and goal for the form in a few words, then paste your clinical notes. No patient name. No date of birth. No health card number. Just the clinical story: diagnosis, disease state, prior treatments, rationale. forMaD knows which evidence to pull, which insurer criteria apply, and what makes this case compelling to reviewers.

Notes processed in a PHI-secured environment
Step 03
Review, verify, download.

forMaD generates a complete, evidence-based form with real trial citations, specific stopping criteria, and proper dosing references. You review every field. You fill in the patient details by hand. You sign. The form is yours. We handled the writing.

Physician reviews and signs everything
Built around one rule

The AI never learns who your patient is.

This is not a compliance add-on. It is the design. Patient names, dates of birth, and health card numbers stay on paper, where they belong. The AI gets the clinical story. You keep the identity.

De-identified by design You paste clinical context, not patient records. Diagnosis, disease state, treatment history. That is all the AI ever sees.
Your data stays private Your notes are processed in a PHI-secured environment, adhering to HIPAA and PHIPA standards. They are never used to train any AI model.
Nothing retained after your session No clinical data is stored by forMaD. When you close the browser, it is gone.
You sign everything forMaD drafts. You review. You complete the patient details by hand. You submit. The clinical judgment is always yours.
