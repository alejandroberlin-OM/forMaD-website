4. AI features

4.1 How drafts are produced. When you ask for a draft, the Service sends your Clinical Content and form inputs to a third-party AI model provider, which returns proposed text and field values. The Service may also: suggest published literature and check suggested references against public bibliographic databases; flag fields that may need your attention; and shorten text to fit a form field. Our Privacy Policy names our AI provider and describes what it receives.

4.2 Limits of AI. Output is generated by probabilistic models. It can be wrong, incomplete, out of date, inconsistent with your notes, or worded in a way that overstates or understates a patient's condition. Suggested references may be inaccurate or may not support the statement they are attached to, even after our automated checks. The same input can produce different Output at different times. We do not review Output before it is shown to you.

4.3 Mandatory professional review. Before any Completed Form is signed, sent or relied on, the Physician must read every field and attached statement, correct anything inaccurate, confirm each statement reflects the Physician's own professional opinion and the patient's record, and verify any cited reference. Flags and checks in the Service are aids; the absence of a flag does not mean a field is correct.

4.4 Decisions remain human. The Service does not make decisions about patients or any other individual. Whether to complete a form, what it says, and whether to sign and submit it are always the Physician's decisions.

4.5 Telling patients. You are responsible for deciding whether and how to tell patients, and the recipients of your reports, that you used AI-assisted software to prepare a document, in line with your regulatory authority's guidance and any applicable law. Some regulatory authorities require a physician to identify who assisted in preparing a third-party report; you are responsible for meeting that requirement.

4.6 Changes to models and providers. We may change the AI models, providers, prompts and checks we use, and the Service may switch automatically to another model from the same provider while the usual model is unavailable. We will update our Privacy Policy, and give notice under section 13.1 where the change affects how Health Information is handled.

4.7 No model training on your information. We do not use Clinical Content, Patient Details or other Health Information to train AI models, and our agreements with our AI providers do not allow them to do so. Schedule A sets out what we may do with De-identified Data.
